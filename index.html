Sentry Sepolia · Reactive Omni

On-chain wallet alerting, without a bot to host.

Sentry watches erc-20 transfers out of a guarded wallet. When a transfer exceeds the threshold, a reactive contract triggers a cross-chain callback that records an alert on the destination chain and pushes a notification. No server, no hosted private key, no polling loop of your own.
